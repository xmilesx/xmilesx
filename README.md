# Miles Gray

I'm a licensed general contractor in Washington, DC, and I build AI tools and agent systems for construction and education.

I ran BBCDC, a DC design-build firm, from 2016 on. We did historic renovation, underpinning and structural work on jobs of $400K to $700K. Before and alongside that I spent about 20 years founding and running small businesses, and I hold a medical degree (DPM, Temple). Now I build the systems I wish I'd had on the job: AI that reads plans and paperwork, prices work, and runs a business in the background.
